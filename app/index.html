    Roadmap to the platinum

    Five stages, in the order to play them. Story first, side characters second, partner stunts third, collectibles fourth, money and 100% last. Nothing after Stage 1 can be missed except the Chapter 1 meditation.

    Missable timeline

    There is no mission replay in GTA VI, so keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission. The game has 15 manual save slots.

      Things that look missable but are not

      • Optional missions that disappear after a while — there are far more than the 100 the checklist needs.
      • Quest givers who go away mid-story (Kenton, for example) — every trophy-related character comes back.
      • Some fences and magazine collectibles in story buildings — replacements unlock, and the checklist does not need all of them.
      • Strangers & Freaks — all strands are still there after the story in free-roam.

      Avoid the Irredeemable criminal profile: it is permanent, and police shoot on sight far more often. It does not block any trophy, but it makes everything harder.
